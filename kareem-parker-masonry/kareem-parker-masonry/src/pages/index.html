FAQs

Common questions

Which areas do you cover?{{icon:plus}}

I’m based in Hamilton and work across the city and surrounding area. Not sure if I cover you? Call or text {{site.phone}}.

Do you really work with my budget?{{icon:plus}}

Yes. Tell me what you’d like to spend and I’ll explain what can be done for that amount, and what can wait.

Do you take on small repairs?{{icon:plus}}

Yes. A few loose bricks or one cracked step is worth fixing early, before frost makes it bigger.

Why should I fix small cracks now?{{icon:plus}}

Water gets into cracks, freezes in winter and expands. Each freeze-thaw cycle makes the crack bigger, so small repairs now save bigger costs later.

Do you clean up after the job?{{icon:plus}}

Always. Full clean-up is part of every job: debris removed, mortar splashes cleaned and your property left tidy.
